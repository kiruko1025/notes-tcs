# Denotational Semantics

operational = transition system
denotational (compositional) = math (not syntax)

## Semantic brackets

### Arithemetic
$$
[]_a : AExp \to Env \to Int
$$

$$
[x]_a \sigma = \sigma(x)
$$

$$
[n]_a \sigma = n
$$

$$
[a_1+a_2]_a \sigma = [a_1]_a \sigma + [a_1]_a \sigma
$$

$$
<a,\sigma> \downarrow_a n \iff [a]_a \sigma =n
$$

### Boolean

$$
[]_b: Bexp \to Env \to \{true,false\}
$$

### Command

$$
[]_c: Command \to Env \partialto Env
$$

Identity function
$$
[skip]_c \sigma = \sigma
$$

Function composition
$$
[c_1;c_2]\sigma = [c_2]([c_1](\sigma)) = ([c_2]_c \circ [c_1]_c)(\sigma)
$$

$$
[x:=a]_c \sigma = \sigma[x\mapsto [a]_a \sigma]
$$
