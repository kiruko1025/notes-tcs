## Evaluation Strategies

$$
(\lambda x.\lambda y.y)\Omega
$$

can be reduced to $\lambda y.y$ (normal) or $(\lambda x.\lambda y.y)\Omega$ (applicative)

1. L -> R or R -> L (doesn't matter really)
2. Top-down (outermost) or bottom-up (innermost)

Normal order = L -> R outermost
Applicative order = L -> R innermost

Applicative is actually more common

Theorem: If a lambda term e has a normal form normal order reduction will produce it.

Normal order + don't reduce under $\lambda$ = call by name
Applicative order + don't reduce under $\lambda$ = call by value
