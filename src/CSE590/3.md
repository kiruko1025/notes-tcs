## $\eta$ - reduction

$$
\lambda f.\lambda y. fy
$$

The redex is $\lambda y.fy$, reduce to $f$

$$
\lambda x.ex -> e
$$

also called extensionality 

## $\omega$ term
$\beta$ reduce to infinite loop
$$
(\lambda x.xx)(\lambda x.xx) -> (xx)[x->\lambda x.xx] = (\lambda x.xx)(\lambda x.xx)
$$

## Semantics

$$
eval: \lambda -term \to \text{Normal Form}
$$
a partial function


### Confluence (Church-Rosser)

If e could reduce to $e_1$ and $e_2$, then there exist some $e_3$ s.t. $e_1$ and $e_2$ can both be reduced to.

1. Normal forms if they exist are unique.
2. Each sequence of different evaluation order will reconcile (doesn't mean a reduction will terminate)

Example:
$$
(\lambda x.x)((\lambda y.y)z)
$$

this reduce to $z$ no matter how you reduce it.

$$
(\lambda x.xx)((\lambda y.y)z) \to (\lambda x.xx)z \to z((\lambda y.y)z) \or ((\lambda y.y)z)((\lambda y.y)z)
$$

## Church Encodings

### Booleans

true:= $\Lambda x \Lambda y.x$
false:= $\Lambda x \Lambda y.y$

### if

if:= $\Lambda b. \Lambda t. \Lambda f. btf$

if true $\inn$ f -> t
if false $\inn$ f -> f

### logics

and := $\Lambda b_1.\Lambda b_2$ if $b_1 b_2$

### Natural Numbers

$$
\bar{n} = \Lambda f. \Lambda x. f^n x
$$

Succ = $\Lambda n. \Lambda f. \Lambda x. f(nfx)$

$$
Succ \bar{n} \to \Lambda f. \Lambda x.f (\Lambda f. \Lambda x. f^n x)fx \to \Lambda f. \Lambda x. f^{n+1}x
$$

### arithmetics

add:= $\Lambda n. \Lambda m. n Succ m$

mult:= $\Lambda n. \Lambda m. n(add m) \bar{0}$

### Pairs

Let $(x_1, x_2) = e_1 \text{ ; } e_2$
Pair = $\Lambda abf.fab$
First = $\Lambda p.p(\Lambda ab . a)$
Second =  $\Lambda p.p(\Lambda ab . b)$

### Let

Let x = $e_1$ ; $e_2$ = $(\Lambda x.e_2)e_1$
